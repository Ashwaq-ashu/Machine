# 🚗 Used Car EDA → ML Price Predictor

> From messy car listings to a machine that learns how much a car might be worth.

---

## 👋 Hey, welcome!

Buying a used car sounds simple...

You look at the **brand**, **year**, **kilometers driven**, **fuel type**, **transmission**, and finally...

💰 **the price.**

But when you have thousands of cars sitting inside a dataset, things get interesting.

Which brands appear the most?

Does an older car really cost less?

Does higher mileage always mean a lower price?

Do automatic cars actually cost more?

And the big question...

> **Can we teach a machine to predict the asking price of a used car?** 🤖

That's what this project is growing into.
